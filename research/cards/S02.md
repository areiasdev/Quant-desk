# S02 - Five-day range breakout, trailing exit

**Status:** PASSED every LAB gate (paper only). **Rank:** 2 of 40. **Score:** 13/25. Source: `research/lab/full/S02.json`, `research/lab/results_all.csv`; code `src/lab/`.

**Idea (one line):** Trade a 4h close outside the last 5 days' range on rising volume, in the breakout direction, and trail out on a close back through the 10-bar extreme.

## Why the edge should exist, and who loses money to it
A multi-day range stores resting stops just beyond its edges; a 4h close outside it on rising volume fills those stops as market orders and attracts trend followers. The losers are range traders positioned for the range to continue, and late shorts who are forced to cover in a squeeze (on the long side, longs also COLLECT negative funding during squeezes, as in MYX in 2025-09).

## Exact rules (as tested; RADAR's default parameters, no tuning)
- Universe: monthly top-10 USDT-M crypto perps by trailing-30-day quote volume (as S16).
- Bars: 4h, anchored 00/04/08/12/16/20 UTC. HH(30)/LL(30) = highest high / lowest low of the previous 30 bars (excluding the signal bar). QVavg(30) = mean quote volume of the previous 30 bars. ATR14 = simple mean of the last 14 true ranges.
- Entry long: 4h close > HH(30) and quote volume >= 1.5 x QVavg(30); short: close < LL(30) with the same volume rule. Market order at the next 4h open. One position per symbol.
- Stop: entry -/+ 2 x ATR14, fixed. Gap through the stop -> exit at that open.
- Exit: first 4h close below LL(10) (long) / above HH(10) (short) -> exit at the next open; time stop: the open 30 bars after entry.
- Costs in every number below: taker 0.05%/side (maker 0.02% for resting targets), slippage 1bp BTC/ETH, 3bp other core, 10bp memes, real funding at every settlement held, delisted coins included (forced close at the last real trade -2%, ESTIMATE).
- Parameters: `{"n": 30, "vol": 1.5, "exit_n": 10}`.

## Size per trade at EUR100 (~$110)
- Risk per trade: 1% = about $1.10. Median stop distance OOS: 4.77% -> median position notional $23 (= $1.10 / stop distance), capped at 3x equity ($330); total open notional is also capped at 3x equity.

Any quarter losing more than 20R, or the short side continuing to lose (already -0.109R/trade OOS).

- Any look-ahead leak? None found. Signals use closed bars only (HH/LL exclude the signal bar), orders fill at the next bar's open, funding is the last SETTLED rate known at the bar close, OI is the 5-minute row inside the bar, the universe for month M uses only bars closed before M. Engine shift tests and same-bar stop/target tests pass (`tests/test_lab_engine.py`).

## WHY THIS COULD FAIL
- 68% of the OOS profit came from ONE coin (MYXUSDT, 2025-09) and the best single trade made +179.1R, including a huge funding credit. Without it, double-cost expectancy is +0.045R.
- OOS closed-trade drawdown was 63% at 1% risk per trade; with up to 12 positions open at once the account is effectively levered to the whole market. That is deeper than the owner's 50% ruin line, measured from the peak.
- The second OOS year lost money: -0.050R/trade, -50.0% at 1% risk, max DD 61%.
- Shorts lost OOS (-0.109R/trade over 503 trades); only longs made money (+0.677R).
- Longest losing streak OOS: 23 trades (walk-forward 30): very hard to follow on a monthly check.
- Monte Carlo bootstrap: 5% worst-case drawdown 59%, P(ruin) 4.1%.
