# S19 - Sell-off into support on rising OI squeeze

**Status:** KILLED - walk-forward OOS not positive. **Rank:** 3 of 40. **Score:** 18/25. Source: `research/lab/full/S19.json`, `research/lab/results_all.csv`; code `src/lab/`.

**Idea (one line):** Buy a top-10 coin that sold off 5%+ in a day into a new 7-day low while open interest rose 8%+, if the bar closes back above that low.

## Why the edge should exist, and who loses money to it
Rising open interest into a sell-off means new shorts are opening late, at support. If the low holds (the bar closes back above the 7-day low), those late shorts are trapped and their covering lifts price. The late shorts lose.

## Exact rules (as tested; RADAR's default parameters, no tuning)
- Universe: monthly top-10 USDT-M crypto perps (as S16). Bars: 1h.
- OI = open interest in contracts (base units), as-of the 5-minute row inside the bar (row stamped at the bar close excluded).
- Entry (long only): ret(24) <= -5%, OI[t]/OI[t-24] - 1 >= +8%, low[t] < LL(168) and close[t] > LL(168) (LL excludes bar t). Market at the next 1h open.
- Stop: low[t] - 0.5 x ATR14. Target: 2R (resting limit, maker). Time stop: 24 bars.
- Costs in every number below: taker 0.05%/side (maker 0.02% for resting targets), slippage 1bp BTC/ETH, 3bp other core, 10bp memes, real funding at every settlement held, delisted coins included (forced close at the last real trade -2%, ESTIMATE).
- Parameters: `{"ret": 0.05, "oi": 0.08, "tR": 2.0}`.

## Size per trade at EUR100 (~$110)
- Risk per trade: 1% = about $1.10. Median stop distance OOS: 4.23% -> median position notional $26 (= $1.10 / stop distance), capped at 3x equity ($330); total open notional is also capped at 3x equity.
- Minimum notional (ESTIMATE: $5 most perps, $20 ETHUSDT, $100 BTCUSDT): 95% of OOS trades are placeable at EUR100. Not placeable: {'SIRENUSDT': 2, 'LABUSDT': 2, 'MYXUSDT': 1, 'ZECUSDT': 1}. Verdict: tradeable at EUR100 (ESTIMATE).
- Up to 2 positions were open at once in the OOS window; about 5.5 trades per month; median hold 4 h.
