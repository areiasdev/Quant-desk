# S16 - Paid to hold: negative funding in an uptrend

**Status:** PASSED every LAB gate (paper only). **Rank:** 1 of 40. **Score:** 16/25. Source: `research/lab/full/S16.json`, `research/lab/results_all.csv`; code `src/lab/`.

**Idea (one line):** Buy a top-10 coin in a 4h uptrend while shorts are still paying longs to hold (negative funding), and get out when the trend or the funding turns.

## Why the edge should exist, and who loses money to it
When price trends up but funding is negative, the crowd on the perp is net short against the trend (or hedged with stale shorts). Longs are paid funding while they wait, and each push higher forces some of those shorts to cover, adding buying. The people who lose are trend-fighting shorts who pay funding *and* get squeezed; secondarily, cash-and-carry desks whose basis trades flip negative pay the funding we receive.

## Exact rules (as tested; RADAR's default parameters, no tuning)
- Universe: each calendar month, the 10 Binance USDT-M crypto perps with the highest quote volume over the 30 days before the month starts (TradFi/index/stablecoin perps excluded; must have traded in the last 24h). Only new entries need membership; an open position may run past month end.
- Bars: 4h, anchored 00/04/08/12/16/20 UTC, complete bars only. SMA = simple average of 4h closes; ATR14 = simple mean of the last 14 true ranges.
- F8 = the latest SETTLED funding rate x 8 / its funding interval in hours, as known at the 4h close (no predicted funding).
- Entry (long only): at a 4h close, close > SMA50 > SMA200 and F8 < 0. Buy at market at the next 4h open. One entry per uptrend leg: after a signal, the setup re-arms only after a 4h close below SMA50 (a signal is consumed even if it was not taken). Never enter on a bar with zero trades. One position per symbol.
- Stop: entry - 2 x ATR14 (ATR of the signal bar), fixed, resting stop-market. If a bar opens below the stop, exit at that open.
- Exit: (a) the first 4h close below SMA50 -> sell at the next 4h open; or (b) the first funding settlement with F8 > +0.01% (seen at a 4h close) -> sell at the next 4h open. No profit target, no time stop.
- Costs in every number below: taker 0.05%/side (maker 0.02% for resting targets), slippage 1bp BTC/ETH, 3bp other core, 10bp memes, real funding at every settlement held, delisted coins included (forced close at the last real trade -2%, ESTIMATE).
- Parameters: `{"fast": 50, "atr": 2.0, "fexit": 0.0001}`.

## The condition that kills it
Long-only trend following: it loses in BTC bear months (OOS bear-month expectancy -0.314R). A sustained bear market, or a structural change that keeps funding positive in every uptrend (no signals, or signals only at tops), kills it. Stop paper-trading it if the drawdown passes 30% of peak equity or the rolling 100-trade expectancy after costs goes below zero.

## First sign it is weakening
Rolling 12-month expectancy below +0.05R after costs, a losing streak longer than 15 (walk-forward longest), or a drawdown beyond 28% (Monte Carlo 95th percentile). The second OOS year (+0.051R/trade) was already at the first threshold.

## WHY THIS COULD FAIL
- The OOS profit is front-loaded: year 1 +0.442R/trade, year 2 +0.051R/trade. The walk-forward profit leans on ONE quarter: 2025-05..2025-07 made +61.0R, the other 11 test quarters together -6.5R; 6 of 12 test quarters lost money.
- It is long-only. In bear months it lost (-0.314R/trade). Year 2 beat BTC only because BTC fell (-29.3% spot-like); in year 1 it made +43.8% while BTC made +55.6%.
- Win rate is 24%: profits rely on a few large trend legs (best trade +17.6R). Missing a handful of them (downtime, a skipped signal) changes the result materially.
- At EUR100, BTC signals cannot be placed (ESTIMATE $100 minimum notional > the position size): 13% of OOS trades are not placeable, so the live trade list differs from the backtest; the tradeable-only row is an approximation at fixed $110 equity.
- Funding history ends 2026-10-31; the behaviour of funding (the input that defines the signal) after that is untested.
- Minimum notional, lot size and leverage tiers are ESTIMATES (exchangeInfo blocked).
