## Method notes and limitations

- Point-in-time universe: meme flag, listed >= 30 days, 30-day mean daily quote volume >= 20M USDT (mean 25.1 eligible memes per day in dev OOS, 7.1 in dev IS), complete day. Rankings use closed days only; orders fill at the next 1h (or 4h) open. Shift tests for the ranking and the edge monitor: `tests/test_lab_rotation.py`.
- The dev run loaded no bar or funding settlement at or after 2026-05-01; the holdout ran once afterwards with the frozen defaults (`holdout.json`).
- Minimum notional: owner's exchangeInfo values (BTC 50 USDT / 0.001 BTC, ETH 20, default 5); memes use the default 5 USDT, an ESTIMATE for memes. With 1% risk at 110 USDT (1.10 USDT at risk) any stop wider than ~22% cannot be placed.
- Equity for sizing is closed-trade equity; drawdowns and worst months are mark-to-market at daily closes (intraday swings are larger). Monte Carlo compounds trades one after another, so it understates overlapping-position drawdowns.
- Delisted memes are included; a position open at delisting is closed at the last real trade minus 2% (ESTIMATE). Hype proxies are exchange data only (no news archive).
- Regime months in dev OOS: bull 7, bear 5, sideways 6.
- Bias disclosure: LAB had seen meme results for 2024-11..2026-10 before this design (see DESIGN.md). R3 (M11) and R5 (short the faders) were informed by that, so their holdout is not fully clean.
